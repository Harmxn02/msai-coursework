\subsection{Lijst van problemen}

\subsubsection{Technical}

\begin{itemize}
	\item Opaque ensemble, no XAI methods applied
	\item No reasoning chain
	\item Proportionality assessment impossible without understanding the basis of a recommendation
	\item Violations of P-01 (transparency) and P-03 (accountability)
	\item Compounded by CH-06 (lack of technical understanding of those who make the decisions) and CH-09 (absense of legal framework) (\citet{khan2021ethicsaisystematicliterature})
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Socio-technical}

\begin{itemize}
	\item Palestinians are indirect stakeholders who were never consulted $\rightarrow$ VSD failure
	\item Assymetry of consequencesbetween operators and Palestinians
	\item Commanders lack sufficient information and meaningful freedom of action $\rightarrow$ violation of two conditions of Meaningul Human Control (\citet{mhcof})
	\item 20-second review window makes critical evaluation impossible
	\item Automation bias, rubber-stamping
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection{Governance}

\begin{itemize}
	\item No Article 36 review because Israel has nto ratified Protocol I
	\item No ethical impact analysis done before deployment (for exampel VSD)
	\item Responsibility is fragmented, no clear accountability chain
	\item No real-time IHL compliance monitoring
	\item No post-deployment audit or incident review process
	\item Lavender extension has 10\% error tolerance $\rightarrow$ civilian casualties inevitable
\end{itemize}
