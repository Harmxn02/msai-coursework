Israel, which allocates 8.8\% of its GDP to defense~\citep{sipri2025milex}, invests heavily in the development of autonomous weapon systems (AWS), with their Prime Minister announcing a \$100 billion investment in homegrown arms production last December~\citep{netanyahu2025arms}. Israel's most well-known AWS is the Iron Dome air defense system, which intercepts incoming projectiles in real time.  Building on this foundation in artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted defense technology, Israel has expanded into offensive targeting applications such as ``Habsora''.
